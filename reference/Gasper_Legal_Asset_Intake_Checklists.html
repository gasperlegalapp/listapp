Asset, Income & Budget Checklists

The official firm checklists for the assets we handle most, plus every ward’s income and monthly budget. Each one walks you through what we need to know, in the order you'll usually find it out, so nothing gets missed between the first phone call and the BusinessMap card.

One sheet per asset.

Two cars means two vehicle sheets. A brokerage account and a checking account at the same bank are two financial sheets.

Mark “Unknown” instead of leaving it blank.

A blank looks like nobody asked. Unknown tells the next person it still needs an answer.

Never write down secrets.

No full account numbers, PINs, passwords, lockbox or alarm codes, or crypto seed phrases. Write where the information is kept, not the information itself.

Get it in writing.

Balances, insurance status, and payoffs told to you over the phone get confirmed by statement, letter, or email before they go on an inventory.

Nothing moves without a record.

Any item, key, title, vehicle, or animal that changes hands gets logged: what, who, when, and where it went.

Red box items come first.

Each sheet opens with the things that can’t wait. Do those before anything else and flag the attorney on any you can’t complete.

Know where the records are.

Every sheet asks what records we have and whether they’re in the hard file, scanned into Actionstep, or both.

Every asset ends “Settled.”

Settled means 100% done: nothing left to do and we’ll never touch it again. Until then it stays open on BusinessMap.

On screen, fill in a sheet and use Copy for BusinessMap to get a clean summary for the asset card. Print any sheet blank or filled in.
